# Raising Reimagined

**A no-cost, vertically integrated fundraising platform that connects suppliers, organizations, and their supporters — helping mom-and-pop shops compete with big retailers by giving them the online tools they can't build themselves.**

🔗 [raisingreimagined.com](https://raisingreimagined.com) · [Live App](https://app.raisingreimagined.com)

---

## The Origin Story

My husband grew up in a wholesale retail business — now in its 3rd generation, run by him and his two brothers. The business also does fundraising with local schools, and we saw firsthand how small local suppliers get crushed by big retailers in the fundraising space. They can't build online platforms, they can't compete on distribution, and they lose out even when their products are better and their relationships are stronger.

We co-founded Raising Reimagined initially to solve that problem for the family business. But once we built the tool, we realized every mom-and-pop shop in the community had the same problem. So we opened it up — and it took off.

## The Problem

Traditional school fundraising is broken for everyone. Organizations spend months coordinating paper catalogs, collecting cash, and managing supplier logistics manually. Small local suppliers have no efficient way to reach school communities. Families are stuck with overpriced products from big national catalogs. And the fundraising organizations themselves lose a huge cut of their profits to middlemen.

We asked: what if we built a platform where organizations keep 100% of their fundraising profits, local suppliers get online storefronts they couldn't build themselves, and families actually want to buy what's offered?

---

## The Solution

Raising Reimagined is a no-cost, vertically integrated fundraising platform. We don't take any of the fundraising organization's profits — that's the core differentiator.

The platform connects three sides:

**For Suppliers (especially local businesses):** A dedicated online microsite with product listings, inventory management, and direct access to school and organization communities. This gives mom-and-pop shops the e-commerce presence they can't afford to build on their own. Stripe Connect handles all payments and splits automatically.

**For Organizations & Teams:** Create fundraising campaigns in minutes. No paper, no cash collection, no logistics headaches. A branded storefront with curated products from vetted local suppliers — and you keep all your fundraising profits.

**For Supporters (Families & Community):** Browse products from trusted local suppliers, purchase directly, and know the money goes back to the organization. No more door-to-door selling or overpriced catalogs.

What that looked like in practice:

- Defined the product architecture: multi-tenant SaaS with role-based dashboards, Stripe Connect payment flows, and supplier microsites
- Wrote all product requirements and managed the development partnership through two major phases (MVP → Phase 2.0)
- Designed the go-to-market strategy targeting PTO networks and supplier partnerships
- Built the competitive positioning against legacy fundraising platforms

## Product Timeline

**Phase 1 — MVP (Dec 2022 – Easter 2023)**
Core marketplace functionality: school storefronts, supplier listings, Stripe Connect payments, basic admin dashboard. Launched beta with initial school partners for Easter 2023 fundraising season.

**Phase 2.0 — Scale (2023 – 2024)**
Multi-campaign support, enhanced supplier microsites, seller dashboards, expanded analytics, mobile optimization, sweepstakes feature, and growth to ~5,000 users. In 2024: 198 campaigns, $127K raised.

**Phase 3.0 — Full Platform Rebuild (Current)**
Currently in a total rebuild of the platform to support the next stage of growth.

## Product Thinking

**User Research → Architecture:** Started with surveys and interviews with PTO leaders. The #1 pain point wasn't "we need better products to sell" — it was "we spend 40 hours per campaign on logistics." That insight shaped the entire platform architecture around automation and self-service.

**Multi-Sided Marketplace Design:** The hardest product challenge was balancing four distinct user types with different needs. Suppliers need inventory tools and reach. Schools need simplicity and trust. Families need convenience. Admins need control. Every feature decision had to consider all four perspectives.

**No-Cost Model:** The biggest product decision was the business model itself. Most fundraising platforms take a cut of the organization's profits. We chose not to. That meant the payment architecture had to work differently — Stripe Connect handles money flows between supporters, suppliers, and organizations without us skimming from the fundraising side. This was a critical trust and competitive positioning decision.

**Phased Rollout:** Deliberately launched MVP for a single fundraising season (Easter 2023) to validate with real campaigns before investing in Phase 2.0 features. Used real campaign data to prioritize the Phase 2 roadmap.
